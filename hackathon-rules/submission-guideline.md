Inside the Game: Developer Hackathon
Microsoft and the Premier League invite developers worldwide to go beyond the scoreline and reimagine how football can be understood, experienced, and shared.

Build an AI-powered solution that transforms synthetic, football-realistic data into explainable match intelligence, real-time narratives, automated recaps, and personalized experiences for fans, studios, broadcasters, and streaming platforms.

Football is more than a final score. Every pass, shot, tackle, change in possession, and shift in pressure can reveal a deeper story about the match.

Inside the Game: Developer Hackathon challenges you to build an AI-driven application or agent that turns synthetic football events into useful, understandable, and engaging match intelligence.

Important data requirement

Projects must use synthetic, football-realistic data. No real Premier League match data is redistributed to participants.

What to Submit
Your submission must include:

Include a newly built Project that meets the project requirements.
Include a Project Pitch or Description: A short elevator pitch for your Project including what you built, which Microsoft and Azure technologies used, what it does, what problem it solves.
Include a demonstration video of your Project. The video portion of the Submission must:
Be less than two (2) minutes in length. Judges are not required to watch beyond two (2) minutes;
Include footage that shows the Project functioning on the device and platform for which it was built;
Provide a public URL to your demo video on YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook Video, or Youku, on the project page on the Hackathon Website; and
Not include third party trademarks, or copyrighted music or other material unless the Entrant has permission to use such material.
Provide a URL to your GitHub code repository. Your repository must be public for your entry to be considered.

3. Real-World Impact & Applicability (20%)

Significance of the problem being solved
